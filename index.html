Questions

Before you
call us.

How is an architect’s fee decided?

Usually as a percentage of construction cost or a rate per square foot of covered area. We quote in writing after the first site visit, once we know the plot, the brief and the level of finish.

How long does the design take?

Indicatively, a 10 marla house takes three to four weeks for concept and 3D, then four to six weeks for approval and working drawings. Larger or commercial projects take longer.

Do you handle building approvals?

Yes. We prepare the drawings and submission for the local building authority and answer their objections, so you are not running between offices.

I live abroad. Can you manage the build?

Yes. We run the project over video calls and WhatsApp, with weekly site photos, a written site report and a sign-off before each payment stage.

Will you supervise construction?

Yes. Supervision is where drawings turn into walls. We visit weekly, check levels, reinforcement and materials, and keep a site diary you can read at any time.

Can you renovate an existing house?

Yes. We measure what is there, keep what is sound and redraw the rest, often adding a floor, a jali front or a courtyard.
